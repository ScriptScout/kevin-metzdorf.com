AI Policy

This policy outlines how I leverage AI-powered tools to support the creation of concepts, copy, designs, and code within my workflow.

Usage of AI

AI-based systems are used exclusively as assistive tools for brainstorming, analysis, structuring, and increasing efficiency. No automated decision-making takes place.

The final review, evaluation, and approval of all content, concepts, and technical implementations are always performed by me personally as the responsible director.

Data Privacy & Security

Only the data strictly necessary for the respective task is used. Personal, confidential, or security-sensitive client data is never entered into public or uncontrollable AI systems.

Quality Assurance

All AI-generated content is professionally audited, refined, and evaluated for technical accuracy, clarity, consistency, and alignment with project goals before being implemented or delivered.

Transparency

Upon request, I provide transparent information about the nature and extent of AI usage within the context of a specific project.

Contact

For any questions regarding the use of AI in our collaboration, please feel free to reach out at info@kevin-metzdorf.com.
